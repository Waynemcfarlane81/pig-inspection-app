      Medications

      Each medication you add here appears in the medication drop-down for every area.
      Withdrawal periods for known medications come from UK/EU product information (pig meat and offal, label dose). Always check your product label or your vet's prescription – periods differ between brands, doses and off-label use.

        Rodent poisons

        Each poison added here appears in the Poison name drop-down in Rodent control.
          Report straight away – do not treat
          Fever with pigs off feed, red or dark skin (especially ears and snout), several sudden deaths, or blisters on the snout, mouth or feet can be a notifiable disease such as African or classical swine fever. Call the Defra Rural Services Helpline on 03000 200 301 (England) and your vet. Do not move pigs.

          Symptoms & dosage guide

          Pick what you are seeing and the pig's size. The guide shows which medicines in the reference list are licensed for that kind of problem, with the label dose worked out for the pig's weight.
          This is a guide only, not a diagnosis. Most of these medicines are prescription-only (POM-V): use them only as your vet prescribes – your vet may choose a different medicine, dose or longer withdrawal period. Doses are from product labels; always read the label before use, and split large volumes between injection sites as the label directs.
